Welcome to Evidentia!

Evidentia is your AI-powered financial document intelligence system. It automatically extracts, validates, and risk-scores invoices so you can focus on what matters.

📤

Step 1 — Upload an Invoice

Go to Upload in the sidebar and drop a .txt or .pdf invoice file. Evidentia handles both formats automatically.

⚙️

Step 2 — Run Batch Process

After uploading, go to Batch Process and click Run Pipeline. Evidentia will extract fields, validate totals, and score each invoice for risk.

📊

Step 3 — Review Results

Head to Invoices to browse all records. Filter by HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW risk. Export to CSV anytime.

🛡️

Step 4 — Check Audit Logs

Every action is recorded in Audit Logs. Track who processed what and when — full transparency and explainability built in.

🚀

You're all set!

Start by uploading your first invoice. If you ever need this tour again, click Help in the sidebar. Good luck!
